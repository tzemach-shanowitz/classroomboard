Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Using it without an account

Your settings, class lists, schedules and links stay in this browser on this device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Using it with an account

If you sign in with Google or email, your classroom setup is saved to your private account in Google Firebase so you can use it on other devices. Only you can read or write it. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Google Drive

Connecting Drive is optional. ClassroomBoard asks for read-only access, so it can show your files and can never change or delete them. The access lasts about an hour and is not kept on a server.

Student names

Class lists are stored in your browser, or in your account if you are signed in. Use first names or nicknames if you prefer. On a shared computer, sign out and clear this device when you finish.

Cookies and tracking

ClassroomBoard has no ads and no analytics trackers. It uses browser storage to remember your settings and, when you sign in, to keep you signed in.

Delete your data

In Settings, open Account and choose "Delete my account and data", or clear this device. Deleting your account removes your saved classroom from the cloud.
