Quick answers

How do I start?

Choose Try for free. No account is needed. Use the toolbar at the bottom to open the timer, whiteboard and other tools.

Where is my classroom saved?

In this browser until you sign in. Sign in (Settings, then Account) to keep it and use it on other devices.

How do I add students?

Open the name picker and choose Add students, or go to Settings, then Classroom.

How do I change the toolbar or background?

Open Settings (the gear), then Toolbar or Appearance.

How do I use it on a smart board?

Open the same link in the board's browser and press the full-screen button. If an update does not show, fully close and reopen the page once.
