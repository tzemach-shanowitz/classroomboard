ClassroomBoard — Google Slides Swipe Navigation

I want to significantly improve the presentation experience in ClassroomBoard, particularly when opening Google Slides presentations connected to my personal Google account.

1. Natural swipe navigation

When presenting on my SMART Board, I want to be able to:

* Swipe my finger to the right anywhere on the slide to advance to the next slide.
* Swipe left to return to the previous slide.
* Navigate without having to press the arrows at the top.
* Experience smooth, natural slide transitions, similar to swiping through pictures on an iPhone.
* Customize the swipe direction for English, Hebrew and Yiddish presentations.

2. Google Slides integration

This must work with Google Slides presentations saved in my personal Google Drive account.

* Keep presentations private.
* Maintain the connection to the original Google Slides file.
* Automatically retrieve updated presentations when reopened.
* Avoid requiring manual downloads or PDF conversions.
* Preserve original slide quality, animations, transitions and embedded media wherever technically possible.

Investigate the best supported integration to achieve this, considering Google’s restrictions on controlling embedded presentations.

3. Writing and annotation

Make sure swipe navigation does not interfere with writing on the presentation.

* When using a finger in navigation mode, swiping should change slides.
* When using a pen or annotation mode, gestures should draw rather than change slides.
* Make switching between navigation and annotation effortless.
* Preserve annotations when moving between slides.

4. Presentation appearance

Make the presentation viewer exceptionally clean and beautiful.

* Full-screen presentation mode.
* Optional navigation arrows.
* Optional slide counter.
* Automatically hide unnecessary controls.
* Smooth transitions.
* Maximum available screen space.
* Easy access to ClassroomBoard tools while presenting.

5. Device compatibility

Support SMART Boards, including their standalone built-in browsers, as well as computers, phones and tablets.

Investigate whether swipe navigation can also control Google Slides during the proposed wireless screen-sharing feature. If this requires a separate secure remote-control mechanism, explain the limitations before implementing it.

6. Testing

Test actual private Google Slides presentations, touchscreen gestures, annotation compatibility, full-screen behavior and presentation updates.

Do not compromise privacy or existing Google Drive functionality.

The ultimate goal is to stand in front of my SMART Board and naturally swipe through my personal Google Slides presentations without touching navigation buttons.

Make the entire experience as seamless, beautiful and intuitive as possible.
