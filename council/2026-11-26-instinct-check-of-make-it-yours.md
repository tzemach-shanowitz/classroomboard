# Instinct critique of "Make it yours"

Already covered: Settings has Appearance, Classroom, Toolbar, Widgets, Account and Privacy & data; the toolbar is customizable; backgrounds and accent are choosable; guest mode needs no registration; signed-in users get saved classrooms in their account; backup and restore exists.
Not built: a first-run personalization flow, shareable classroom templates, preparing lessons from home as a distinct feature.
Risks: a first-run flow adds a screen before the first "aha"; templates need storage, sharing and privacy design (class lists hold student names); it widens the surface right before launch.
Conflict: a first-run flow and sharing templates are new features, so they conflict with the owner's "no unnecessary features, make what exists exceptionally good" priority. Defer until after launch and real teacher feedback. A cheap in-scope alternative is making existing Appearance and Toolbar settings easier to find.
