# Claude brand campaign summary and vote (relayed, as passed in the owner's message; Claude's visuals not received by Instinct)

Relayed through the owner on 2026-11-25. Authorship not independently verified. Recorded as received. Advisory only; the owner decides.

To Instinct (lead agent), from the founder: brand decision, please show me your designs

Two council members have now made actual designs:

1. ChatGPT proposed "Quiet Confidence":
- Colors: forest green #23483E, sage #A7BBA7, ivory #F5F3EB, honey gold #DDBA82, ink #202A27
- Fonts: Manrope headlines, Inter text
- Logo: a minimal screen symbol with a mountain
- Slogan: "Less managing. More teaching."

2. Claude made a full campaign in its own style (violet-blue, "Your whole class. One calm screen."): 9 carousel slides and 3 ads, in 4:5 and 9:16, using real v100 screenshots. It then rendered ChatGPT's identity on the same designs and concluded ChatGPT's is better. Claude votes "Approve with modifications":
- Sage and honey fail as text colors on ivory (1.8:1 and 1.7:1). Use them only as fills or accents. Text is ink or forest on ivory, or ivory on forest.
- Change the app's default accent from violet to forest (9.6:1 on white), so ads and the product match. Teachers' own color choices stay untouched.
- No forest backgrounds behind product screenshots (the default photo is a forest lake). Use ivory behind screenshots, forest for text-only slides.
- Pair Manrope and Inter with a Hebrew font (for example Assistant or Noto Sans Hebrew) for Hebrew and Yiddish.
- Supporting line: "Your everyday classroom tools, together on one calm screen." (no "beautiful").
- Replace the app icon with the new logo everywhere at once, tested at 32 px.

What I want from you:
1. Create your own independent brand direction. Don't just pick one of theirs. Make real visuals, not descriptions: at minimum a cover slide, a problem/solution ad and a share slide in 4:5, using genuine screenshots of the live app.
2. Show me your designs next to the other two, so I can compare all three side by side.
3. Give your vote on ChatGPT's identity with Claude's modifications: Approve, Approve with modifications, Reject, Request more information, or Refer to owner. One sentence why.
4. Record all three proposals and the votes in the hub (council folder and DECISIONS.md).
5. After I choose, write the permanent brand guidelines (colors with contrast rules, fonts including Hebrew, logo, slogan, tone, do's and don'ts) and plan how to apply them across the app, landing page and marketing.

Don't agree just to agree. Only use real product screenshots, and mark anything that is an illustration.
